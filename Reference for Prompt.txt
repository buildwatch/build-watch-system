Development and Testing
       The investigators initiate their probe by forming a team consisting of scrum masters, developers, a writer, and individuals with expertise in customer collaboration. They have optimized the team's performance and efficiency by promoting order, productivity, and collaboration while also eliminating any obstacles that may impede development. In addition, they have overseen the daily scrum sessions to track the team's progress. Researchers collaborate with their clients to discuss the functionality of the online system, determining which aspects to include in the suggested project. The scrum team and scrum master work closely together to effectively accomplish daily development activities and project documentation. Ensuring the timely delivery of suitable resources in a methodical and gradual manner guarantees a smooth workflow and swiftly addresses any issues that may occur.
       The researchers have identified and extensively examined the web app catering management system to formulate a proactive strategy and objective. The online solution implemented the concepts of the Scrum Model, whereas the researchers utilized a descriptive methodology. The system includes features that allow users to classify, rank, and graphically depict sales and items, thereby mitigating issues such as stacks of paperwork and manual ordering. The web system utilizes interactive visual representations, such as dynamic graphs, tables, and charts, to provide comprehensive sales patterns and market insights. The researchers purposefully designed the system to be straightforward and easy to navigate, using user-friendly interfaces and well-structured layouts to ensure smooth operation. The researchers carefully choose visual characteristics, such as color coding and graphing styles, in order to highlight important findings. The system focuses primarily on data analysis and visualization capabilities, which enable users to discover and comprehend trends and patterns within the order process. The researchers do test and evaluation to verify the reliability, effectiveness, and user-friendliness of the data visualization, showcasing its precision and interoperability across different browsers and devices. Before starting the creation of the project system, the researchers consulted their capstone adviser and clients to determine the most favorable components that meet the needs of the system's users.

Implementation Plan
Pre - Implementation Plan
       The researchers performed an initial investigation and interview for their three Caterspot clients. All of Sta. Cruz Laguna’s catering services benefit from this business to increase consumer traffic and business marketing. The three clients that the researchers were investigating all had issues with manually maintaining their records, performance, and sales tracking. The researchers came up with a creative idea to build a website that would be simple for people to access, browse, and include the capabilities they would require for their business.

During Implementation
       The researchers presented the web-based catering management system with analytics to customers and clients. They demonstrated how the system could assist in managing sales and reservations. The researchers also asked if the system required further enhancements. Additionally, they noted that the client’s feedback would help improve the system.
 
Post Implementation
       Alongside the data entry during implementation, the database stored information collected during the system setup phase. After deployment, the researchers take charge of maintaining the system to ensure it operates smoothly. Ongoing efforts are essential to preserve its reliability and usability. They monitor performance, promptly address any issues, and apply necessary updates or improvements to ensure the system remains efficient and dependable over time.
