## Development and Testing

The investigators initiate their probe by forming a team consisting of scrum masters, developers, a writer, and individuals with expertise in customer collaboration. They have optimized the team's performance and efficiency by promoting order, productivity, and collaboration while also eliminating any obstacles that may impede development. In addition, they have overseen the daily scrum sessions to track the team's progress. Researchers collaborate with their clients to discuss the functionality of the online system, determining which aspects to include in the suggested project. The scrum team and scrum master work closely together to effectively accomplish daily development activities and project documentation. Ensuring the timely delivery of suitable resources in a methodical and gradual manner guarantees a smooth workflow and swiftly addresses any issues that may occur.

The researchers have identified and extensively examined the Build Watch LGU Project Monitoring System to formulate a proactive strategy and objective. The online solution implemented the concepts of the Scrum Model, whereas the researchers utilized a descriptive methodology. The system includes features that allow users to monitor projects, track progress, manage RPMES forms, and coordinate between different government units, thereby mitigating issues such as stacks of paperwork and manual project tracking. The web system utilizes interactive visual representations, such as dynamic graphs, tables, and charts, to provide comprehensive project insights and monitoring data. The researchers purposefully designed the system to be straightforward and easy to navigate, using user-friendly interfaces and well-structured layouts to ensure smooth operation. The researchers carefully choose visual characteristics, such as color coding and graphing styles, in order to highlight important findings. The system focuses primarily on project management and monitoring capabilities, which enable users to discover and comprehend trends and patterns within the project lifecycle. The researchers do test and evaluation to verify the reliability, effectiveness, and user-friendliness of the system, showcasing its precision and interoperability across different browsers and devices. Before starting the creation of the project system, the researchers consulted their capstone adviser and clients to determine the most favorable components that meet the needs of the system's users.
